Step 10 language audit status
=============================

Purpose and scope
-----------------
This artifact records the current status of the tracked-file language guard.
The candidate set is the local tracked tree returned by the NUL-delimited
equivalent of `git ls-files -z`; untracked and ignored filesystem content is
outside the audit scope.

File handling
-------------
Tracked text is audited regardless of filename extension, including dotfiles,
SVG, PyInstaller specifications, and extensionless scripts. Supported text
encodings are UTF-8, UTF-8 with BOM, UTF-16 LE with BOM, and UTF-16 BE with
BOM. Known binary content receives an explicit binary exclusion. Missing,
malformed, or unsupported tracked content produces a path-specific audit
error instead of being silently skipped.

Language policy
---------------
The guard detects Norwegian characters, high-signal Norwegian vocabulary,
and controlled English terminology that no longer belongs in project-owned
prose. Narrow exceptions cover only exact detector vocabulary fixtures,
intentional Unicode Person names, and verified machine, protocol, legal, or
technical values. No audit script, audit test, fixture file, or directory is
exempt as a whole.
